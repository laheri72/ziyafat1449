## 2026-07-04 - Missing ARIA Labels on Header Icons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like the menu toggle) and search inputs often rely purely on visual cues (FontAwesome icons and placeholders). Adding aria-labels and hiding the icons (aria-hidden) ensures screen readers understand the functionality.
**Action:** Always verify that every input has an explicit label (or aria-label) and every icon-only button is properly tagged with an aria-label and title.
